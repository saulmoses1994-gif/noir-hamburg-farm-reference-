## Structural Observations

- **All 8 pages share the same content pattern:** short intro → 4-8 H2 sections → 4-5 FAQ items. Word counts cluster **around 350-450 words** — well below the 1200-2500 that Google typically rewards for cornerstone commercial pages in a competitive Hamburg escort vertical.
- **No H3 subheadings anywhere.** Section text runs as flat prose, hurting scannability and long-tail keyword coverage.
- **Meta descriptions are on the short side** on 4 pages (113-119 chars) — losing SERP real estate; 130-155 is the sweet spot.
- **Two title lines exceed 60 chars** (Business @81, Event @69, Luxury @66, Travel @66) — will be visually truncated in SERPs.
- **Internal linking is asymmetric.** VIP + Business + Event + GFE + Travel are each linked from every /escort/{district} area page (16-24 backlinks). Luxury is unusually well-linked from blog articles (11 blog inbound), but Hotel is under-linked (16 total, weakest of the 8). No page has an inbound reference from the /faq page or /escort-hamburg intro cards to itself with descriptive anchor text — most are the generic navigation card text.
- **All 8 pages emit exactly the right schema trio:** `Service` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList`. Consistent and validation-clean.
- **The 3 requested slugs that don't exist** — high-class-escort-hamburg, elite-escort-hamburg, dinner-date-escort-hamburg — are gaps worth filling. High-Class and Elite are both premium-tier terms with legitimate search volume; a Dinner Date URL (which currently 404s) should redirect to `/services/dinner-companion-hamburg` OR the slug should be renamed. The current title of the dinner page is already "Dinner Date Hamburg" so the slug mismatch is confusing for both SEO and users.
